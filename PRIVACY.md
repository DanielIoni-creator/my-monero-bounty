## How Monero Protects Your Privacy

### 🔐 Monero's Built-in Privacy
- **Stealth addresses** - Each payment goes to a one-time address
- **Ring signatures** - Hides which output is being spent
- **RingCT** - Hides the transaction amount
- **Kovri** (optional) - Hides your IP address

### 🛡️ Our Practices
- We never share your Monero address with anyone
- We never ask for personal information
- We don't track transactions after payment
- We don't log IP addresses
