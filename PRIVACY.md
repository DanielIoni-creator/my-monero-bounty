## How Monero Protects Privacy

- **Stealth addresses** - One-time addresses per payment
- **Ring signatures** - Hides which output is being spent
- **RingCT** - Hides transaction amounts

## Transparency Without Sacrifice

- ✅ Amounts paid are public (accountability)
- ❌ Who received payments is private
