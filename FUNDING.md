## 🔒 Privacy First

### What's Visible vs Hidden

| What's Visible | What's Hidden |
|----------------|---------------|
| Amount sent on blockchain | Who sent it (stealth addresses) |
| Date/time of transaction | Who received it (stealth addresses) |
| Transaction ID | Your wallet balance |
| | Your IP address (with Tor/i2p) |

### Our Privacy Commitment

- ✅ We only need your Monero public address
- ✅ We don't ask for name, email, or KYC
- ✅ Your address cannot be linked to your GitHub identity
- ✅ You remain anonymous if you choose
- ✅ No personal information is shared with anyone

### Transparency Without Sacrificing Privacy

We balance transparency with privacy by:
1. **Publicly recording** amounts paid out (accountability)
2. **Not recording** who received them (privacy)
3. Using **Monero's privacy features** (stealth addresses, RingCT)

## 📋 Verification

All payouts are recorded on the Monero blockchain.
Transaction amounts are public, but sender/recipient remain private.
